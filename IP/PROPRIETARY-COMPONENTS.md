# Proprietary and Protected Components

The following categories are treated as protected research/IP subject matter unless an explicit license states otherwise: original source code; original specifications and schemas; original architecture diagrams and system-boundary definitions; original experimental protocols; original evidence and provenance methodologies; original reconciliation and lineage mechanisms; original repair and review workflows; original benchmarking/evaluation methodologies; original documentation and explanatory material.

Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.

Historical artifacts retain their original provenance and licensing status.

This document does not itself establish trade-secret status, patent issuance, or patent-pending status.
