# Architecture IP Map

## Lineage
Original system-of-systems → KCN — Ketchum's Cognitive Network → MANIFEX — Engineering Intelligence & Execution OS → KSI / Super Intelligence Under Human Authority → Evolutionary Architecture → EA-G1 → EA-G2 → Kronos-X

## Subject areas
KCN: cognitive/intelligence capability architecture and associated experimental mechanisms.

MANIFEX: engineering intelligence, execution, provenance, integration, and research infrastructure.

KSI: governance, epistemic boundaries, human authority, qualification/evidence separation, and constitutional controls.

EA-G1 / EA-G2: evolutionary mechanisms for preserving historical records while allowing new interpretation and evidence.

Kronos-X: automated experimental-laboratory orchestration, exact source pinning, isolated execution boundary, continuous lineage, reconciliation, evidence packages, reproducibility, diagnosis, repair traceability, independent review, and qualification separation.

## Historical/evolved distinction
The fact that a concept appears in a later architecture does not establish that it existed in the same form in an earlier implementation. Historical records retain their original status.
