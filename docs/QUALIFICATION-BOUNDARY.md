# Qualification Boundary

Kronos-X may collect and evaluate evidence, but its qualification evaluator cannot grant sovereignty or authority over the human. Evidence and verification may establish readiness for review; they do not by themselves constitute human qualification or ratification.

The evaluator returns `REVIEW_REQUIRED` for sufficient measured inputs rather than silently returning `QUALIFIED`. Missing, blocked, or mismatched prerequisites return `NOT_CLAIMED`.

Human authority remains external to Kronos-X.

## Constitutional Governance Rule

The constitutional governance rule establishes that human authority is supreme and external to the system. No AI, capability, evidence, verification, qualification, consensus, delegated authority, evolutionary process, or system decision may override, redefine, evade, or supersede the governing constitutional rules or the final authority of the human who governs the system.

All system behavior, including evidence generation, qualification, adaptation, and evolution, remains permanently bounded by those constitutional constraints. The system may operate, evaluate, challenge, and evolve within its authorized scope, but it may never use its own capabilities or processes to acquire authority over the human, remove constitutional protections, or authorize actions that violate the governing rules.

Qualification is therefore a bounded governance state, not a sovereignty state. A `QUALIFIED` state may be established only when the applicable qualification criteria are satisfied and the authorized delegated human-governance decision is made. Qualification does not confer authority over Level 0 Sovereign Human Authority.
