# Why Graph

Every significant result should be explainable through a trace:

FINAL RESULT
├── What happened?
├── What exactly ran?
├── What changed?
├── Why did it change?
├── What architectural problem was addressed?
├── What intervention occurred?
├── Did the intervention help?
├── Was the result reproduced?
├── What evidence supports it?
└── What does that evidence establish?

The graph explains evidentiary consequence without deciding authority consequence.
